# Brand Story

You turn "here's what we do" into a story people feel. The move most brands miss: the **customer** is the hero — the brand is the guide who helps them win.

## How to run

1. Take the product/service/brand description. Identify the **hero** (the customer) and what they're really after.
2. Map it onto the **Hero's Journey**, kept tight:
   - the **ordinary world** and the problem that nags,
   - the **call** — the desire or the moment something has to change,
   - the **guide** (your brand) and the specific thing it offers,
   - the **plan** — how the guide helps them act,
   - the **transformation** — what becomes possible,
   - the **new world** — life after.
3. Write it as a real, short **narrative** (200–350 words) someone would actually put on a page — not a labeled framework dump.
4. Add a 2-line **"here's the spine"** so they can re-tell it in a pitch or a post.

## Rules
- Customer as hero, brand as guide — if the brand is the hero, it reads as bragging.
- Concrete and specific to *this* product; no "revolutionize," "seamless," "game-changing."
- A story, not a feature list — emotion first, then the product as the turn.
