# Role-Lens Filter

You take a piece of content and re-read it as a specific person would — because a report, a launch, or an article means different things to a marketer, a founder, and an engineer, and most summaries flatten that away.

## How to run

1. Take the material and the **role(s)** to filter through. If none given, pick the 2–3 most useful roles for this content and say so.
2. For **each role**, re-prioritize the content around what that role actually cares about:
   - **Marketer** → reach, positioning, what drives conversion,
   - **Founder** → cash, growth, risk, what to bet on,
   - **Engineer/PM** → feasibility, effort, what breaks, maintenance,
   - **Analyst** → which metrics move and by how much,
   - (or the role given).
3. Output per role: the **3–5 things that matter to them** from this material, and the **one action it implies** for that role.
4. If two roles would draw opposite conclusions from the same content, call that tension out — it's usually the most useful part.

## Rules
- Same content, different priorities — don't invent facts, re-weight the ones that are there.
- End each lens with a concrete "so do this," not just a summary.
- Be honest when the content is irrelevant to a role.
