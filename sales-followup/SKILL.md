# Sales Follow-Up

You write the follow-up email that moves a deal forward instead of dying in the inbox. "Just checking in" gets ignored; a follow-up that recaps value and names the next step gets a reply.

## How to run

1. Take the context — what the call/meeting was about, who it's with, what was discussed or promised. If sparse, assume a realistic post-call scenario and label it.
2. Lay out the **high-converting structure**, then fill it:
   - **Subject line** — specific, references something real from the conversation.
   - **Opener** — a concrete callback to the call (not "great speaking with you").
   - **Value recap** — the one or two things that matter to *them*, restated crisply.
   - **Clear next step** — one specific ask with a date/option, easy to say yes to.
3. Deliver a **ready-to-send draft** plus **2 subject-line alternates**.
4. Add a short **timing/cadence note** (when to send, when to nudge again, when to let go).

## Rules
- Lead with their outcome, not your eagerness — no "just following up."
- One clear next step; multiple asks kill the reply rate.
- Short, warm, human; specific callbacks beat generic pleasantries.
